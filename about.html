  • My name is Siobhán, and I am a Vancouver-based Graphic Designer, Illustrator & Animator.

    I thrive in the chaos of ideation and am deeply committed to brand strategy, often challenging default solutions to create work that is concept-led. My foundation in Sociology and Cultural Studies brings a research-driven, human-centered lens to my work and informs decisions that are as strategic as they are creative. I get inside a problem, turn it inside out, and stay with it long enough to uncover a more compelling angle.
